Fine hair and thin hair are often used interchangeably, but they describe two different aspects of your hair. Fine hair refers to the diameter of each individual strand, while thin hair usually refers to the amount or density of hair on the scalp. Understanding the fine hair vs thin hair difference can help you identify your hair type and choose a routine that suits your needs.
