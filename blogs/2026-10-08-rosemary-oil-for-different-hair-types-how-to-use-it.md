Rosemary oil is a popular ingredient in hair and scalp care, especially among people looking to build a simple routine around scalp health. But using rosemary oil effectively is not just about choosing the ingredient. Your hair type, hair density, scalp condition, and how easily your hair becomes oily or weighed down can all affect how you should use it.
