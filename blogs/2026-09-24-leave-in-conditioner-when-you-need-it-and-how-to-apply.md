A leave-in conditioner is a hair care product designed to stay on your hair instead of being rinsed out. It can help make hair easier to detangle, improve softness, control frizz, and provide lightweight moisture throughout the day. Unlike a regular conditioner, which is washed out after a few minutes, a leave-in conditioner remains on the lengths and ends until the next wash.
