Salicylic acid is a popular skincare ingredient for managing excess oil, clogged pores, blackheads, and acne. It is available in different formulations, including face washes and serums. While both can help with acne-prone skin, they differ in how they are used, how long they stay on the skin, and how they fit into a skincare routine.
