Having an oily scalp and dry hair ends at the same time can be confusing. Your roots may look greasy soon after washing, while the lengths and ends feel rough, frizzy, or brittle. This combination is often caused by differences in how oil moves along the hair shaft, along with hair type, washing habits, heat styling, and chemical treatments.
