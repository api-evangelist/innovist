Healthy hair is usually smooth, manageable, and resilient. When hair is repeatedly exposed to heat, chemical treatments, friction, or environmental stress, the hair shaft can become weaker and more difficult to manage. This can lead to damaged hair, which may look dry, rough, dull, frizzy, or be more prone to breakage Understanding the signs of damaged hair can help you identify hair damage early and adjust your routine before the problem gets worse.
