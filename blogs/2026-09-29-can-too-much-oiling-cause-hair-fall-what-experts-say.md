Hair oiling can help reduce dryness and make hair feel softer, but using too much oil or applying it too frequently may not suit every scalp. If you are wondering whether too much oiling causes hair fall, the answer depends on your scalp type, hair condition, and how often you oil. A balanced routine can help keep your scalp clean and your hair healthy.
