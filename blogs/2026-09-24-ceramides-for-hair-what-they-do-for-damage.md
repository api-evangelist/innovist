If your hair feels dry, rough, frizzy, or damaged, ceramides may help improve its overall condition. Ceramides are lipids that can help support the hair’s protective surface, improve smoothness, and help reduce moisture loss. They can be especially useful when hair has been weakened by heat styling, chemical treatments, or environmental stress.
