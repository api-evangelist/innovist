Oily skin can often look shiny, especially around the forehead, nose, and chin. Excess oil may also make pores appear larger and contribute to clogged pores and breakouts. While cleansing can remove surface oil, maintaining a balanced skincare routine is important for managing oily skin without stripping away its natural moisture.
