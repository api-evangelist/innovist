Frizzy hair can look dry, rough, puffy, and difficult to manage. If you often wonder why your hair is so frizzy, factors like dryness, humidity, heat styling, hair damage, and your natural hair type could be responsible. Understanding the causes of frizzy hair can help you choose the right routine, instead of using the same products and techniques for every hair type.
