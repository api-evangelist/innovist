Acne-prone skin often comes with excess oil, clogged pores, redness, and marks left behind by breakouts. Choosing skincare products that address these concerns without making the skin feel dry or irritated can be challenging. Niacinamide is a popular skincare ingredient that can help manage excess oil, support the skin barrier, and improve the appearance of uneven skin tone.
