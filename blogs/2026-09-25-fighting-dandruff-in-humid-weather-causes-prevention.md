If you notice your dandruff getting worse in humid weather, you are not alone. Many people notice increased scalp buildup, visible flakes, and oily roots during humid weather. The combination of heat, humidity, and increased sweating can make the scalp oilier and may worsen dandruff and scalp irritation.
